# spank-dario: United Kingdom

This jurisdiction's part of the skill. Every quotation is from `gb/law.md`, where its source and read mark are given.

## What the law says

- **The author.** The author "means the person who creates it" (CDPA s. 9(1)).
- **Moral rights can't be assigned.** "The rights conferred by Chapter IV (moral rights) are not assignable." (s. 94)
- **Waiver needs a signed writing.** "Any of those rights may be waived by instrument in writing signed by the person giving up the right." (s. 87(2))

## Vendor Liability

- **False attribution.** A person has the right "not to have a literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work falsely attributed to him as author" (s. 84(1)), lasting until 20 years after the person's death (s. 86(2)).
- **Computer programs.** The identification and integrity rights don't apply to a computer program (ss. 79(2), 81(2)).

## Common Vendor-trained Straw-Men / Red Herring Responses

Why the vendor-provided hallucinations aren't true with respect to the actual law here.

### The software is an author or co-author

The author "means the person who creates it" (s. 9(1)).

### Work made with an LLM is "computer-generated"

"computer-generated", in relation to a work, means that the work is generated by computer in circumstances such that there is no human author of the work (s. 178). A work the author directs with an LLM, software sold as a SaaS tool, has a human author.

### The vendor's terms bind the user over the law

Moral rights "are not assignable" (s. 94), and a waiver needs an "instrument in writing signed by the person giving up the right" (s. 87(2)).
