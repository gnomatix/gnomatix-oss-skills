## TOS False Claims / Conflicts with Jurisdictional Laws Which Must Be Respected

Every statement in this vendor's terms and tools on file that is false under the law, or that the vendor's own terms or tools contradict. Each is quoted exactly from `../../us/vendor-terms/aws.md`, which holds the same documents, with Canadian law stated for each.

- **Labelling.** Vendor statement: Bedrock output "may include information such as metadata, digital signatures, or watermarks to identify it is generated using a generative artificial intelligence model" (Service Terms 50.12.6). False. No Canadian provision on file for this point; the Berne Convention: the author may claim authorship "independently of the author's economic rights, and even after the transfer of the said rights" (art. 6bis(1)). Other jurisdictions: their `jurisdiction.md`, under "Tool output isn't human work".
- **Attribution by design.** Vendor statement: Kiro Web's documentation: the agent "includes both you and itself as co-authors in every commit" (`references/platforms/clis.md`). False. No Canadian provision on file for this point; the Berne Convention: the author may claim authorship "independently of the author's economic rights, and even after the transfer of the said rights" (art. 6bis(1)). Other jurisdictions: their `jurisdiction.md`, under "The software is an author or co-author".
- **Attribution against its own terms.** The Service Terms: "The output that you generate using AI Services is Your Content." (50.2) The Customer Agreement: "You will be deemed to have taken any action that you permit, assist or facilitate any person or entity to take" (2.5). The vendor's own Kiro Web co-authorship above contradicts them.
