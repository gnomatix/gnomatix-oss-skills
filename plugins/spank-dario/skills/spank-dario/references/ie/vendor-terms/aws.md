## TOS False Claims / Conflicts with Jurisdictional Laws Which Must Be Respected

Every statement in this vendor's terms and tools on file that is false under the law, or that the vendor's own terms or tools contradict. Each is quoted exactly from `../../us/vendor-terms/aws.md`, which holds the same documents, with Irish law stated for each.

- **Labelling.** Vendor statement: Bedrock output "may include information such as metadata, digital signatures, or watermarks to identify it is generated using a generative artificial intelligence model" (Service Terms 50.12.6). False. Irish law: "author" means "the person who creates a work" (s. 21). "computer-generated" means "the author of the work is not an individual" (s. 2), and work the user directs has an individual author. Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "Tool output isn't human work".
- **Attribution by design.** Vendor statement: Kiro Web's documentation: the agent "includes both you and itself as co-authors in every commit" (`references/platforms/clis.md`). False. Irish law: "In this Act, "author" means the person who creates a work" (s. 21). Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The software is an author or co-author".
- **Attribution against its own terms.** The Service Terms: "The output that you generate using AI Services is Your Content." (50.2) The Customer Agreement: "You will be deemed to have taken any action that you permit, assist or facilitate any person or entity to take" (2.5). The vendor's own Kiro Web co-authorship above contradicts them.
- **Attribution against its own policy.** The Responsible AI Policy prohibits use "for intentional disinformation or deception". The vendor's own Kiro Web co-authorship misrepresents the authorship of the user's work.
- **License.** Vendor statement: "we may use and store AI Content that is processed by each of the foregoing AI Services to develop and improve the applicable AI Service and its underlying technologies" (50.3). False. Irish law: moral rights "shall be incapable of assignment or alienation" (s. 118), and a waiver "shall be in writing and signed by the person waiving the right concerned" (s. 116(2)). Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The vendor's terms bind the user over the law".
- **Choice of law.** Vendor statement: "The laws of the State of Washington" (11.4). False. Irish law: moral rights "shall be incapable of assignment or alienation" (s. 118), and a waiver "shall be in writing and signed by the person waiving the right concerned" (s. 116(2)). Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The vendor's terms bind the user over the law".
