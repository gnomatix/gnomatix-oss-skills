## TOS False Claims / Conflicts with Jurisdictional Laws Which Must Be Respected

Every statement in this vendor's terms and tools on file that is false under the law, or that the vendor's own terms or tools contradict. Each is quoted exactly from `../../us/vendor-terms/anthropic.md`, which holds the same documents, with Irish law stated for each. This file's entries above give the contracting party and governing law here.

- **Origin.** Vendor statement: "Our Services may generate responses (we call these "Outputs")". False. Irish law: "In this Act, "author" means the person who creates a work" (s. 21). Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The software is an author or co-author".
- **Assent.** Vendor statement: "By accessing our Services, you agree to these Terms." False. Irish law: moral rights "shall be incapable of assignment or alienation" (s. 118), and a waiver "shall be in writing and signed by the person waiving the right concerned" (s. 116(2)). Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The vendor's terms bind the user over the law".
- **Assignment.** Vendor statement: "we assign to you all of our right, title, and interest—if any—in Outputs." False. The vendor holds no right in the user's work, so the "if any" assignment conveys nothing. Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The vendor's assignment means the vendor owned the work".
- **Attribution by default.** Vendor statement: Claude Code adds `Co-Authored-By: <name> <noreply@anthropic.com>` to commits and "🤖 Generated with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code)" to pull requests (settings reference). False. Irish law: "In this Act, "author" means the person who creates a work" (s. 21). Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The software is an author or co-author".

- **Responsibility against credit.** The consumer terms: "You are responsible for all Inputs you submit to our Services and all Actions." The vendor's own default attribution credits its software with that work.
- **Attribution against its own policy.** The Usage Policy, under "Do Not Engage in Deceptive Campaigns or Artificial Activity", bars impersonation "in order to mislead people about their origin or attribution" and content "that misrepresents its origin, authorship, or independence". The vendor's own default attribution misrepresents the authorship of the user's work.

False. Irish law: moral rights "shall be incapable of assignment or alienation" (s. 118), and a waiver "shall be in writing and signed by the person waiving the right concerned" (s. 116(2)).
