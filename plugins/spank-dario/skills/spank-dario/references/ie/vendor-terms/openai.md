## TOS False Claims / Conflicts with Jurisdictional Laws Which Must Be Respected

Every statement in this vendor's terms and tools on file that is false under the law, or that the vendor's own terms or tools contradict. Each is quoted exactly from `../../us/vendor-terms/openai.md`, which holds the same documents, with Irish law stated for each. This file's entries above give the contracting party and governing law here.

- **Assignment.** Vendor statement: "We hereby assign to you all our right, title, and interest, if any, in and to Output." False. The vendor holds no right in the user's work, so the "if any" assignment conveys nothing. Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The vendor's assignment means the vendor owned the work".
- **Human work.** Vendor statement: "Represent that Output was human-generated when it was not." False. Irish law: "author" means "the person who creates a work" (s. 21). "computer-generated" means "the author of the work is not an individual" (s. 2), and work the user directs has an individual author. Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "Tool output isn't human work".
