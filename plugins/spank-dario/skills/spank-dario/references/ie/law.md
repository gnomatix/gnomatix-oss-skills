## Clarification

- "In this Act, "author" means the person who creates a work" (s. 21).
- "The rights conferred by Chapter 7 shall be incapable of assignment or alienation." (s. 118).
- "A waiver made under this section shall be in writing and signed by the person waiving the right concerned." (s. 116(2)).
