## Clarification

- ""author" means a person who creates a work" (art. 2(1)(ii)).
- "Author's moral rights are exclusive to that author, and are inalienable" (art. 59).

Statute: Copyright Act (Act No. 48 of 1970), official English translation, https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/4207 ("Last Version: Act No. 52 of 2021"; the translation is not authoritative). Japanese text: e-Gov, https://laws.e-gov.go.jp/api/1/lawdata/345AC0000000048 (elaws.e-gov.go.jp/document?lawid=345AC0000000048 redirects to laws.e-gov.go.jp; latest amendment listed in the fetched text: 令和八年六月二四日法律第四八号).

- (f) art. 15(2) — read — same URL — "For a work of computer programming that an employee of a corporation, etc. makes at the initiative of the corporation, etc. in the course of duty, the author is the corporation, etc., so long as it is not stipulated otherwise in a contract, in employment rules, or elsewhere at the time the work is made."
