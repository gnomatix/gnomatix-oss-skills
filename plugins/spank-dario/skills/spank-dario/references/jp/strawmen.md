# Japan: Vendor False Claims / Straw Men

Each source is quoted exactly from its primary text. Each concerns works with no human author, or a registration or policy question. None of them describes work an author directs with a software tool, an LLM included. The skill's section "Common Vendor-trained Straw-Men / Red Herring Responses" quotes real instances of their misuse.

- Agency for Cultural Affairs, "General Understanding on AI and Copyright in Japan" overview — read 2026-11-04 — https://www.bunka.go.jp/english/policy/copyright/pdf/94055801_01.pdf — "Materials autonomously generated by AI* are not "creatively produced expressions of thoughts or sentiments" and are therefore not considered (copyrighted) "works." *i.e., material that is generated by AI without any instructions from humans". The document also says "The General Understanding is not legally binding". It concerns material made without human instructions. Misused as: work an author directs with an LLM isn't a work.
