## TOS False Claims / Conflicts with Jurisdictional Laws Which Must Be Respected

Every statement in this vendor's terms and tools on file that is false under the law, or that the vendor's own terms or tools contradict. Each is quoted exactly from `../../us/vendor-terms/anthropic.md`, which holds the same documents, with French law stated for each.

- **Origin.** Vendor statement: "Our Services may generate responses (we call these "Outputs")". False. French law: "La qualité d'auteur appartient, sauf preuve contraire, à celui ou à ceux sous le nom de qui l'oeuvre est divulguée." (CPI L113-1) Other jurisdictions: their `jurisdiction.md`, under "The software is an author or co-author".
- **Assent.** Vendor statement: "By accessing our Services, you agree to these Terms." False. French law: the author's moral right "est perpétuel, inaliénable et imprescriptible" (L121-1). Other jurisdictions: their `jurisdiction.md`, under "The vendor's terms bind the user over the law".
- **Assignment.** Vendor statement: "we assign to you all of our right, title, and interest—if any—in Outputs." False. The vendor holds no right in the user's work, so the "if any" assignment conveys nothing. Other jurisdictions: their `jurisdiction.md`, under "The vendor's assignment means the vendor owned the work".
- **Attribution by default.** Vendor statement: Claude Code adds `Co-Authored-By: <name> <noreply@anthropic.com>` to commits and "🤖 Generated with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code)" to pull requests (settings reference). False. French law: "La qualité d'auteur appartient, sauf preuve contraire, à celui ou à ceux sous le nom de qui l'oeuvre est divulguée." (CPI L113-1) Other jurisdictions: their `jurisdiction.md`, under "The software is an author or co-author".
- **Attribution against its own terms.** The commercial terms: the customer "owns its Outputs" and "Anthropic disclaims any rights it receives to the Customer Content". The vendor's own default attribution above contradicts them.
- **Responsibility against credit.** The consumer terms: "You are responsible for all Inputs you submit to our Services and all Actions." The vendor's own default attribution credits its software with that work.
- **Attribution against its own policy.** The Usage Policy, under "Do Not Engage in Deceptive Campaigns or Artificial Activity", bars impersonation "in order to mislead people about their origin or attribution" and content "that misrepresents its origin, authorship, or independence". The vendor's own default attribution misrepresents the authorship of the user's work.
- **License by claimed assent.** Vendor statement: "We may use Materials to provide, maintain, and improve the Services and to develop other products and services, including training our models". False. French law: the author's moral right "est perpétuel, inaliénable et imprescriptible" (L121-1). Other jurisdictions: their `jurisdiction.md`, under "The vendor's terms bind the user over the law".
