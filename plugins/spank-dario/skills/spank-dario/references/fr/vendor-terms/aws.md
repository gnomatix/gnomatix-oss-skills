## TOS False Claims / Conflicts with Jurisdictional Laws Which Must Be Respected

Every statement in this vendor's terms and tools on file that is false under the law, or that the vendor's own terms or tools contradict. Each is quoted exactly from `../../us/vendor-terms/aws.md`, which holds the same documents, with French law stated for each.

- **Labelling.** Vendor statement: Bedrock output "may include information such as metadata, digital signatures, or watermarks to identify it is generated using a generative artificial intelligence model" (Service Terms 50.12.6). False. French law: "L'auteur d'une oeuvre de l'esprit jouit sur cette oeuvre, du seul fait de sa création, d'un droit de propriété incorporelle exclusif et opposable à tous." (L111-1) Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "Tool output isn't human work".
- **Attribution by design.** Vendor statement: Kiro Web's documentation: the agent "includes both you and itself as co-authors in every commit" (`references/platforms/clis.md`). False. French law: "La qualité d'auteur appartient, sauf preuve contraire, à celui ou à ceux sous le nom de qui l'oeuvre est divulguée." (CPI L113-1) Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The software is an author or co-author".
- **Attribution against its own terms.** The Service Terms: "The output that you generate using AI Services is Your Content." (50.2) The Customer Agreement: "You will be deemed to have taken any action that you permit, assist or facilitate any person or entity to take" (2.5). The vendor's own Kiro Web co-authorship above contradicts them.
- **Attribution against its own policy.** The Responsible AI Policy prohibits use "for intentional disinformation or deception". The vendor's own Kiro Web co-authorship misrepresents the authorship of the user's work.
- **License.** Vendor statement: "we may use and store AI Content that is processed by each of the foregoing AI Services to develop and improve the applicable AI Service and its underlying technologies" (50.3). False. French law: the author's moral right "est perpétuel, inaliénable et imprescriptible" (L121-1). Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The vendor's terms bind the user over the law".
- **Choice of law.** Vendor statement: "The laws of the State of Washington" (11.4). False. French law: the author's moral right "est perpétuel, inaliénable et imprescriptible" (L121-1). Other jurisdictions: their `skill.md`, under "The vendor's terms bind the user over the law".
