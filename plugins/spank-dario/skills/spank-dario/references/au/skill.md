# spank-dario: Australia

This jurisdiction's part of the skill. Every quotation is from `au/law.md`, where its source and read mark are given.

## What the law says

- **Only individuals.** "Only individuals have moral rights." (Copyright Act 1968 s. 190)
- **Moral rights can't be transferred** (s. 195AN(3)), and a consent has effect only if given "in relation to specified acts or omissions" and specified works (s. 195AWA(3)).
- **Computer programs are literary works.** A literary work includes "a computer program" (s. 10(1)), and Part IX on moral rights has no carve-out for programs.

## Vendor Liability

- **False attribution.** "The author of a work has a right not to have authorship of the work falsely attributed" (s. 195AC(1)).
