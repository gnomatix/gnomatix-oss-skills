# Australia: Vendor False Claims / Straw Men

Each source is quoted exactly from its primary text. Each concerns works with no human author, or a registration or policy question. None of them describes work an author directs with a software tool, an LLM included. The skill's section "Common Vendor-trained Straw-Men / Red Herring Responses" quotes real instances of their misuse.

- Copyright Act 1968 (Compilation No. 65, 2 April 2026) — read 2026-11-04 (absence) — https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1968A00063/latest/text — the Act has no "computer-generated" provision: a search of the full text finds no "generated", and no "artificial intelligence". Misused as: Australian law has a special rule for work made with AI tools.
