## TOS False Claims / Conflicts with Jurisdictional Laws Which Must Be Respected

Every statement in this vendor's terms and tools on file that is false under the law, or that the vendor's own terms or tools contradict. Each is quoted exactly from `../../us/vendor-terms/openai.md`, which holds the same documents, with Australian law stated for each.

- **Assignment.** Vendor statement: "We hereby assign to you all our right, title, and interest, if any, in and to Output." False. The vendor holds no right in the user's work, so the "if any" assignment conveys nothing. Other jurisdictions: their `jurisdiction.md`, under "The vendor's assignment means the vendor owned the work".
