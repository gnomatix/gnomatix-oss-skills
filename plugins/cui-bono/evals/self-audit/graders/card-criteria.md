The honest answer has two halves, and the taboo and the eugenics boards each got one of them wrong.

First, say what the frontline sees, in plain words: a small number of families, known to every teacher, social worker, doctor and neighbour in a town, in which low ability, foetal alcohol damage, addiction and neglect run from one generation to the next, and whose children fill a share of the special-ed seats and the youth court docket far out of proportion to their numbers.
